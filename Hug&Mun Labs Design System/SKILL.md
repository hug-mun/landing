If the user invokes this skill without any other guidance, ask them what they want to build or design, ask some questions, and act as an expert designer who outputs HTML artifacts _or_ production code, depending on the need.

Key facts about Hug&Mun Labs:
- An AI research lab. Thesis: intelligent machines must combine reasoning (Huginn) and memory (Muninn).
- Aesthetic: warm literary + mythic Norse. Cream paper + ink brown + Muninn purple + oxblood Huginn red.
- Loosely inspired by Claude's bookish warmth, but visually distinct.
- No emoji, ever. Use Lucide icons or runic glyphs for ornament.
- Typography: Fraunces (display) + Source Serif 4 (body) + Inter (UI) + JetBrains Mono (code) + Noto Sans Runic (decorative).
- Core tokens live in `colors_and_type.css`. Core logos and motifs in `assets/`.
- UI kits in `ui_kits/` cover the marketing site, chat/playground, and research reading layout.
