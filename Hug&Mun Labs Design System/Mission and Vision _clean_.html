Mission & vision.

Mission

Advance how AI systems use representation and state to make reliable and controllable decisions.

Vision

Enable AI systems that maintain internal state and make consistent, controllable decisions over time.

How we work

We design and test structured representations and state mechanisms to understand how they change decision behavior in AI systems. Our work focuses on how semantic state, memory, and belief interact to produce consistent and controllable decisions over time.
